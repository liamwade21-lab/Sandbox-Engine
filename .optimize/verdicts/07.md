## Lead's note on 07-1
Moving the per-frame camera values (blur, blur_delta, prev_view, hide) out of the material assets
into one buffer written in place each frame (a ShaderBuffer or a view-level uniform, as 02-1 does
for terrain light) keeps the shader inputs bit-identical, so the output is unchanged. It is a
moderate refactor of stars.rs and the star / galaxy-far shaders. Listed as recommended (larger).
