The Faulcon DeLacy Sidewinder mod's files: where each came from. Written by copy_sidewinder (src/sidewinder/convert/).
Elite Dangerous is Frontier Developments'; every file below taken from it is theirs (install version 2026.10.23.334925).
Grades are the plan's (docs/spec-mods-sidewinder.md): A = Elite's own bytes, E = recreated.
Elite's original assets come from the decoded export (re/elite/frea_export/sidewinder/decoded, made from the install by the export tool, out of git): its README gives the exact vertex decode and the spaces.
Models: LOD0 only: Elite's LOD switch rule is not known.

Not shipped: the pg_sidewinder_extendedengine drive bolt-ons (the export's engine_left / engine_right: rigs PG_SideWinder_ExtendedEngineLeft / Right, models driveboltonleft, driveboltonright, plateleft, plateright, fin, material SideWinder_DriveBoltOn) are the Proving Ground arena's by the evidence at rest: their pg_ names, as the arena's pg_sidewinder hull and cockpit, and the item database listing them only beside the CQC loadouts.
