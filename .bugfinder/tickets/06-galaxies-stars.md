# 06 Galaxies, star octree, star fields and star clusters

## Paths in scope
- crates/se_core/src/star_octree.rs, proc_galaxy.rs, proc_galsys.rs, galaxy_model.rs (2.3k lines)
- crates/se_engine/src/procstars.rs, procgalaxies.rs, clusters.rs, star_search.rs, stars.rs
- crates/se_engine/assets/shaders/stars.wesl (only for CPU/GPU data layout mismatches)

## What it does
Builds SE's procedural universe: galaxies (positions, types), the octree of procedural stars inside
a galaxy (loaded/generated by octree node as the camera moves), star clusters, the star point
renderer, and the star search (finding the nearest/brightest star, selecting by click).

## Likely bug classes
- Octree streaming: nodes loaded/unloaded at the wrong time, caches that never shrink, a node's stars
  regenerated differently on revisit (determinism), async generation finishing after the camera has
  moved on and being applied to the wrong node.
- Wrong results: star IDs/names not matching SE's (RS numbers), positions in wrong units (pc vs ly),
  galaxy-local vs universe frame, star search returning the wrong star or a barycentre.
- Panics: octree depth overflow, index math, empty nodes, division by zero for zero-size nodes.
- GPU buffers: instance buffers growing forever, CPU struct layout not matching the shader struct.
- Click/selection: picking stars hidden behind UI or at the wrong screen position.
