# 01-018 neutron stars and white dwarfs in the GR shader (done)
- SE: FUN_1404d4100 draws every class-11 star (class word 0x5800; type < 12 white dwarf, 12 neutron star, 13 hole) with FUN_140270900 / einstein.glsl, with the STAR define for non-holes (shader flag bit 1), then its glow (no ordinary sphere/corona). STAR: gr_intersection.glh StarSurface on each chord (gravitational shift with starR = starRadius / starRadiusScale, equator Doppler, Fbm spots moving with starTime = frac(t / (1000 sqrt(R km))) x 256, poles 1.25x, x (1+z)^-1.25 x 0.75), StarParams = (Teff <= 60000 K, R / sphere, equator v/c, 1). White dwarf: gravRadius = R and KerrM = starRadiusScale = Rg / R (local_9e3/local_9c8), render sphere max(3 R, 100 Rg) (FUN_1404d6710). Disks: FUN_140507690 for all class 11 (20% roll without the tag; N(30000, 3000) K; geometry from R, formulas from Rg; white dwarf x = R / Rg, rate temperature FUN_1404dfdc0, jets 3 R x 2.5, no density cap; Teff raised to a hotter disk); volumBright temperature = max(disk, star) (FUN_140270900). KerrSpin defaults FUN_1405045a0: NS N(0.2, 0.05), WD (0.2 u)^2.
- Ported: blackholes.rs (NS/WD in the lens list, unit/KerrM/StarParams, hide_lensed_stars hides their StarSphere + corona and the sprite from 0.5 px), blackhole_params.rs (Remnant kind, resolve/of_star with R and Rg, rate_temperature), accretion_disk.wesl star_surface, blackhole_lens.wesl star hits (centre ray + AA sub-samples). Hook outside my files: lensflare.rs skips stars in LensedHoles (ordered after blackholes::LensSet).
- Checked: Crab Pulsar at 1500 km (no ghost ring after hiding glow/flare, crab_cmp3.png) and 120 km (crab_near.png), Hercules X-1 at 300 km (default disk + jets visible, star seen through the disk), U Gem B at 1.5e6 km (catalog Density 5000: opaque disk and jets, ugem.png).
- Notes: SE's default NS disk is thin (~1e-5 g/cm^3) and faint; SE's equator speed formula gives ~1e-9 c (ported literally). The glow and flare are drawn into the frame before the lens here (SE draws its glow after), hence the hiding.
