# 05-021 auroras as particle sprites (partial: switch and brightness only)
- SE: aurora.glsl sprites from NorthParticles/SouthParticles (catalog writer FUN_1404eb190), centres displaced by Fbm4, top colour from noise, FbmTex brightness drifting with ScaleTime.w, TEX_NOISE / DETAIL by AuroraQuality, no day/night mask; AuroraBright (main-def 1), hdrAuroraBright 1, realAuroraBright 3.
- Ported: ShowAurora (Shift+Ctrl+A) hides them; AuroraBright from main-def.cfg scales them (aurora.wesl, `sun_dir.w`).
- Not ported: the particle-sprite renderer (the VBO generation and the sprite uniforms ScaleTime/SunVecClip were not traced; the port keeps its mesh curtains, their night mask and their x1.5 tuning), AuroraQuality, the photo-mode realAuroraBright.
- README controls: Shift+Ctrl+A auroras.
