# 05-019 eclipse model details (partial: shader model done everywhere, 8 casters and stars only on sky and rings)
- SE: eclipse_common.glh EclipseValue (caster's area share when smaller, else 1; penumbra `maxPhase x smoothstep(1, 0, (d - |rl - rc|)/(rl + rc - |rl - rc|))`; maxPhase `(1 - cos rc)/(1 - cos rl)`, rc^2/rl^2 under 0.001 rad), product over casters, `1 - pow(Shadow, 1/1.6)` (ECLIPSE_SHADOW_GAMMA), scaled by AmbientColor.a (DAT_1419fbb04^2, the EnableEclipses fade; FUN_14044fe70 / FUN_1404e0b50); no red tint; up to 8 casters per light, stars allowed.
- Ported: eclipse.wesl is SE's model (EclipseValue, Auto angle choice, gamma); `eclipse_light` (planet surface and clouds) uses it too, the red umbra tint and the exact disc-overlap area are gone. eclipse.rs: 8 casters per light, stars as casters (a binary companion), per light (the extra stars too) for skies and rings; AmbientColor.a from `RenderSwitches::eclipses` on sky and rings. A cheap reject (behind the point, or wider than r + |v| sin(rl)) skips the trigonometry for most casters.
- Checked: "Triple Eclipse on Jupiter" (.audit/work/rings/jup_triple.png), the 2027 eclipse (c_ecl_space_z.png: darker umbra core, narrower penumbra than HEAD). Unit test of the CPU twin of EclipseValue (eclipse.rs).
- Not ported (terrain.rs, not mine): the surface/clouds casters are still terrain.rs `update_eclipses` (4, main star only, `!b.star`); it could use `eclipse::EclipseCasters` (lights[0..]) instead, and multiply by `RenderSwitches::eclipse_intensity()`. Ellipsoid-scaled positions (05-020). terrain.rs `EclipseUniform::light_at` (CPU twin of the old model) is now only used to gate the sky's sun-disc mask.
