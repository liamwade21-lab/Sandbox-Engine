# 10-015 Ships manager and multiple ships
- SE: seShipsManager with SMTab "My ships" / "Other ships", seShipsManagerBuildDialog (Build menu by class: Astronaut, Probe, Shuttle, Starship, Planetship, Space station, Building, Planetary base; Build, Build fleet), Destroy, Destroy all, Nickname, Take control (of spacecraft), Control remotely, Teleport here; many ships live in seSpacecraftArray (exe utf16 lines 2987-3041).
- Ported (`ship/manager.rs`, in the existing Ships window): the two tabs list every ship (number, nickname, design, distance) with Take control, Control remotely (fly it, camera stays), Teleport here, Destroy, Destroy all, Nickname; "Build menu" toggles SE's class tabs with Build, Build fleet and Fly (build + take control). Ships are kept: `ship NAME` no longer removes the others. Script: `ship build|fleet|other NAME`, `ship take|remote|teleport|destroy N`, `ship destroyall`, `ship nick N TEXT` (N: number or nickname); `unship` still removes all.
- Idle ships hold station: landed ones stay on their body, others keep the place they were left at relative to the body with the nearest surface (turning with it within two radii, fixed axes beyond), rebuilt from the body every frame so they follow it at any time rate; engines and warp off. Only the flown ship runs the physics (unchanged).
- Assumed: fleet of 5 in a V three lengths ahead of the camera; Build places ships ahead of the camera; "Other ships" are the ones scripts build with `ship other`.
- Checked: unit tests (fleet layout, ship lookup, action names); `.audit/work/ships/fleet.png` (list with nickname), `fleet_take.png` (took control of #4), `fleet_ahead.png` (fleet seen from the pilot's seat).
