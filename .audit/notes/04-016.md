# 04-016 catalog asteroid and comet models (done)
- SE: ship.glsl draws body models with the asteroids.sml material: DiffMap x DiffColorScale (+ DiffColorConst), NormMap in the stored tangent frame (binormal w cross(t, n), which runs along -v), detail diffuse `mix(0.5, tex(uv x s), mag)` at s and 4s times (4 + 6 mag^2), detail normals at two scales blended in by DetBumpMag (BlendNormals method 3), bump maps turned to normals (bump_to_norm), Lambert mixed with the Hapke hot spot by AmbientParams.w, a Blinn-Phong highlight (SpecColor, SpecPower), ambient AmbientParams.x.
- Ported: model_script.rs parses every .sml key (`MaterialPreset`, `preset_for_material`); models.rs loads DXT1/3/5 and ATI1/ATI2 DDS as GPU block-compressed with their mips (67P's albedo and normals were white), PNG/JPEG with box mips, BumpMap/DetBumpMap to normals; colour maps read raw (SE's DiffGamma 1: Eros map 0.56 x DiffColor 0.4 = 0.22, 67P 0.43 x 0.10 = 0.04, the bodies' albedos); model.wesl as above with the tangent frame rebuilt per pixel from UV gradients (the stored tangents agree in direction, and their w gives binormal -v on every vertex of Eros/Itokawa/Arrokoth; no 16 B/vertex on 67P's 12 M vertices); body_models.rs takes the body's Hapke/SpotBright/SpotWidth(CB) and SE's AmbientLighting (the port's fixed 0.02 ambient is gone).
- Checked: Eros (eros_new3.png vs eros_base.png), 67P (p67_new3.png vs p67_base.png: was flat white), frame times at 67P 8.7 ms vs 10.6 ms baseline in the same session.
- Not ported: eclipse shadows on models (ECL), thermal glow (THERM), which highlight path (SPEC/ASPEC) SE compiles for these materials is assumed (the diffuse colour as specular colour). Oumuamua has no maps (as in SE's .sml).
- Unit tests: material constants, bump-to-normal orientation, DDS block levels, preset parsing.
