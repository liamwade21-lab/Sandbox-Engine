# 01-010 coronae (done; texture corona not ported)
- SE: FUN_1404d4100 draws the corona of a star (type 3, no NoCorona bit 14 of +0x11c0, pixel radius >= 0.2) with FUN_1405481c0 when +0x1d04 is 0 (procedural; 1 = texture corona FUN_140547110, set only in the star editor). FUN_1405481c0: three perpendicular planes in the body frame, scaled by (1 - oblateness) x Radius, corona_plane_proc.glsl with Shape = (RayDensity, RayCurv, 0.25, 13), Bright = (0.06, 1.5, 10.001, 15) (body +0x1cf0..0x1d00 from FUN_1404d1970), EyePos.w = fract(days / Period x {0.001, 0.0012365131, 0.0007631217}) x 10000 per plane, Color = star colour at unit luma x FUN_1404c07f0(Teff) x Brightness x 2.5 x body Brightness. Corona block keys FUN_14046bfc0/FUN_140468860 (Period years x 365.24218985). Defaults FUN_140507070: Radius rnd_tn(5.5,0.3,4.5,6.5) R*, RayDensity rnd_tn(3.5,..) sqrt(Teff/5880), RayCurv clamp(rnd_tn(15,2,9,21)/sqrt(g/28), 3, 180), Brightness 0.5, Period 10 d (brown dwarfs 1 d, giants/carbon 10 d x R/3.75e7 km, white dwarfs x0.001, neutron x1e-5); Wolf-Rayet 25/20/1; remnants x10; black holes NoCorona.
- Ported: corona.rs + corona.wesl (SE's fbm shader, planes, phases, colour, ellipsoid scale), body_params.rs Corona::resolve / CoronaStar / corona_temperature_bright, NoCorona flag. hdrStarCoronaBright read from main-def.
- Port choice: the planes are cut where the line of sight meets the star's ellipsoid (SE's disc outshines them ~1000x; the port's STAR_SURFACE_BRIGHT disc does not, so they left a seam across it).
- Checked: Sun 5e6 km (.audit/work/flares/c1_sun5m.png), 2027-08-02 totality from Luxor (eclipse_tot_zoom.png), Sirius A (sirius2.png), Betelgeuse (betelgeuse_far.png, RayCurv 180 swirls), ETA Car A (catalog Corona block), oblate test (oblate_test2.png); frame time at the Sun 5e6 km median 10.0 vs 9.6 ms baseline (GPU shared).
- Not ported: the texture corona (StarCoronaTextures, editor only); the renderer factors at +0x3630/+0x35a0 and per-render +0x2a0 (taken as 1); the SE clock epoch (J2000 used, phase only); SE's RNG stream (the port's SeRng from the name).
- README: "Stars: procedural coronae (SE's three ray planes, per-star Corona keys and defaults, NoCorona)".
