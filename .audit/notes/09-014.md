# 09-014 Render resolution, upscaling and sharpening
- SE: hdr_final.glsl `SHARP` variant = `textureFastCAS(Base, uv, LimParams.w)` (FUN_1403238d0 builds the LIMIT_FOV/DITHERING/SHARP variants), `PostEffects` `Sharpness` 1 (FUN_140326220, 0..1). Solids at `SolidResolution` of the window, the shorter side capped at `SolidTargetRes`, even sizes (FUN_1401910f0); `SolidFXAA` fxaa.glsl, then upscale_bicubic.glsl `textureUpscaled` with `SolidUpscaleMode` = 3*METHOD+SHARP (bicubic_common.glh); `SolidSharpness`, or 1 - `Sharpness` when `SolidAutoSharp` (FUN_14018dae0). Keys in FUN_1401933a0; presets High 0.85/1080/8/FXAA, Medium 0.7/9, Low+Potato 0.6|0.5/3, Ultra 1.0/1440/8.
- Ported (step 1): colour correction split into SE's hdr_tone (into the scratch) and hdr_final (CAS when Sharpness > 0, correction, dithering, back): same pass and flip count. Sharpness from main-def, saved in PostEffects, slider in Settings > Graphics > Post-effects; `SE_SHARPNESS`.
- Ported (step 2): `render_scale.rs` + `upscale.wesl`: Bevy's `MainPassResolutionOverride` draws the main pass into a corner of the main texture; before projection/lens/bloom/tone/FXAA, FXAA (or a copy) into a small scratch, all 12 upscale modes back over the main texture (no flip). Graphics presets read the four Solid* keys; Settings > Graphics > Quality has resolution, upscale filter and FXAA-before-upscaling. `SE_SOLID_RES`. docs/render_scale.md.
- Differences: the whole main pass is reduced (SE: solids only; Bevy's main pass cannot be split, stars keep their pixel size via view.viewport); off during the warp pass (reads full-size depth) and cube-map projections; fxaa.glsl's luma read made consistent with its HDR Reinhard (exposure 1), FXAA 3.11 default thresholds (SE's not traced); SHARP chosen when Sharpness > 0 (the draw-time selection was not traced).
- Not ported: VolumResolution/VolumUpscaleMode/VolumBilateral (the volumes are blended in Bevy's main pass; a separate resolution needs their own phase/node), SolidTemporalUpscale (combine_temporal.glsl), checkerboard modes 12/13 (drawn as bicubic CAS).
- Checked: unit tests (sizes, modes, preset keys); 30 paused Earth frames identical apart from the selection pointer at every step; 0.25 and 0.85 screenshots aligned with full resolution. Frame times, same build, old-equivalent (SE_SOLID_RES=1 SE_SHARPNESS=0) vs defaults (High 0.85, bicubic CAS, FXAA, Sharpness 1), median of 300 frames: Moon surface 45.0 / 45.0 ms (CPU-bound), Earth ocean surface 63.4 / 61.6, deep space 4.15 / 4.10. (The agentseed baseline is older than this branch: Earth surface 8.5 ms there against 62.5 here with sharpening off, so it is not comparable.)
