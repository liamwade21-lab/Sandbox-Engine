# 01-011 lens flare occlusion (done)
- SE: lens_flare.glsl samples FrameTex (clamp 0..100, luma limited to BloomDynamicRange = Params2.w) and BloomTex at mip Params2.y at the sun, mixes them by Params2.z, multiplies by smoothstep(3, 10, luma), pow(1/2.2), and scales every sprite by it. FUN_1402698e0: level = clamp(7 - log2(screen height / sun radius px), 0, 5); FUN_1404e6240: mix = smoothstep(LensFlareSmallMethodRadius 5, LargeMethodRadius 8, radius px) (FUN_140029820). Flares are drawn after the bloom textures are made.
- Ported: bloom.rs runs, after the bloom chain and before the combine, a probe pass (lens_flare_probe.wesl, one texel per sun) and then the flare sprites (lens_flare.wesl, a post pass with a vertex buffer instead of a main-pass material) scaled by the probe; lensflare.rs builds FlareDraw (sources, levels, mixes, sprites) and drops the analytic body/horizon/sea occlusion. Sprite colour no longer carries the star colour or FLARE_GAIN 8 (the frame sample gives both, as in SE). No gain: the star discs are on SE's scale (see 01-010), so an open Sun gives modulate ~2 as in SE.
- Checked: Earth orbit (r_earthorbit.png), sunset at 40N 0E with the sun on the horizon (r_sunset_b.png, tinted flare) and set (sunset_c.png), 2027 totality (r_eclipse.png, no flare), the Sun behind Earth's limb (r_limb108.png, matches base); frame times: sunset 16.5 ms vs current main's build 16.7, Sun at 5e6 km 6.2 vs base 6.5 (the agentseed base is 12.1 at the sunset: the gap is main's newer merges, not this work).
- Not checked: a sun behind a mountain or clouds specifically (same path as the horizon case); fish-eye/cubemap mode (not in the port).
- README: "Lens flares are dimmed and tinted by whatever covers the sun (SE's frame/bloom sample)".
