# 01-011 lens flare occlusion (done, with a calibration gain)
- SE: lens_flare.glsl samples FrameTex (clamp 0..100, luma limited to BloomDynamicRange = Params2.w) and BloomTex at mip Params2.y at the sun, mixes them by Params2.z, multiplies by smoothstep(3, 10, luma), pow(1/2.2), and scales every sprite by it. FUN_1402698e0: level = clamp(7 - log2(screen height / sun radius px), 0, 5); FUN_1404e6240: mix = smoothstep(LensFlareSmallMethodRadius 5, LargeMethodRadius 8, radius px) (FUN_140029820). Flares are drawn after the bloom textures are made.
- Ported: bloom.rs runs, after the bloom chain and before the combine, a probe pass (lens_flare_probe.wesl, one texel per sun) and then the flare sprites (lens_flare.wesl, now a post pass with a vertex buffer instead of a main-pass material) scaled by the probe; lensflare.rs builds FlareDraw (sources, levels, mixes, sprites) and drops the analytic body/horizon/sea occlusion. Sprite colour no longer carries the star colour or FLARE_GAIN 8 (the frame sample gives both, as in SE).
- Calibration: the port's discs are STAR_SURFACE_BRIGHT 2 where SE's are hdrStarSurfaceBright 2000 x its Planck table, so the samples are raised by STAR_FRAME_GAIN 10 (lensflare.rs); an open sun then saturates at the limit as in SE, unlit scenery stays below the 3..10 ramp. Remove it if star brightness is ever put on SE's scale.
- Checked: Earth orbit (f4_earthorbit.png), sunset at 40N 0E: sun 4 deg up (sunset_a.png), on the horizon (sunset_b.png, tinted flare), set (sunset_c.png, no flare), 2027 totality (eclipse_tot.png, no flare); frame times equal to baseline within noise (6.4 vs 6.7 ms, 8.8 vs 8.0 ms on two runs).
- Not checked: a sun behind a mountain or clouds specifically (same path as the horizon case); fish-eye/cubemap mode (not in the port).
- README: "Lens flares are dimmed and tinted by whatever covers the sun (SE's frame/bloom sample)".
