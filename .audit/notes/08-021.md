# 08-021 Coordinate grids
SE: FUN_140266a90 draws the celestial grids when ShowGrids (master) and ShowGridEquatorial/Ecliptic/Galactic are on, each by FUN_140266db0 at infinity (rotation-only view) in its frame (FUN_140267b60: identity, obliquity about X, galactic), colour CelEquatorGridColor/CelEclipticGridColor/CelGalacticGridColor x ExposureCalibration/Exposure, alpha colour.a x grid^2 x master^2, prim_line.glsl width LinesWidth. Lines (FUN_140315270 primitives 9/10): parallels every 10 deg -80..+80, meridians pole to pole, 24 (1h) or 36 (10 deg). Labels: each meridian at lat -60..60 step 30 ("%ih" / "%i°"), every 3rd meridian's parallels ("%+i°"). Geographic grid (FUN_1404c7460): the 10-degree grid on each body's ellipsoid in its frame when its radius > 20 px, fading in to 40 px, GeographicGridColor x ShowGridGeographic^2, not under the master. Keys (FUN_1405ba8f0): Ctrl+; ToggleCelGrids (ShowGrids), Alt+; ToggleGeoGrids.
Ported: `grids.rs` (geometry, labels, frames from se_core::coords, geographic grid on SystemBody orient/shape, front side only), switches/keys/fades in `overlay.rs`, Filters toolbar "Celestial grids" button (right click: the four grids) and Settings entries in `hud.rs`, keys saved and scriptable. Paths are culled by the view cone before projecting.
Checked: grids.png (6h meridian through Betelgeuse at RA 5.9h Dec +7.4, ecliptic lat -16, galactic l 200 b -9 as expected; Earth's grid at right), geo.png (Earth from 15000 km), far_c.png (only the equatorial grid from 40 kpc). Unit tests: line/label counts, frames (equinox, obliquity, NGP). CPU 0.9 ms with all three grids and the geographic one on at FOV 70.
Not ported: SE's exact label offsets (it moves labels by a FOV fraction; here a few pixels beside the point), the exposure factor on the colours, the night/VR colour override; the geographic grid is not depth-tested (drawn on the side facing the camera, over terrain).
