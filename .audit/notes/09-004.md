# 09-004 Photo modes and auto exposure
- SE: main-def `PhotoMode` 0 HDR / 1 Manual / 2 Auto (`SwitchPhotoMode`, V). FUN_1404c51a0: exposure = exp2(ExposureComp) * `ExposureCalibration`, times the auto factor in mode 2. FUN_1404c09a0 + hdr_histogram.glsl: 256 bins of log2 luminance over `MinExposureComp`..`MaxExposureComp` (-4..42; the vars at 0x1419b8340/470), bin 0 left out, centre mask lum * `AutoExpMaskValue` * sqrt(1 - r^2/`AutoExpMaskSize`^2), the `AutoExpLow/HighPercent` (10/90) bins give a median; comp = clamp(log2(median/`AutoExpTargetBright` 0.35), `AutoExpMin/MaxExpComp` 0..42), approached by 1 - exp(-dt*speed), `AutoExpSpeedUp` 5 when it grows else `AutoExpSpeedDown` 1.
- Ported (exposure.rs, auto_exposure.wesl): `PhotoMode` resource from main-def (saved in settings), V cycles it with the "Photo mode: X" message (not while flying a ship, whose V it is), Settings > Graphics > Post-effects combo. Auto: compute pass before tone mapping (no post_process_write flip) builds SE's histogram from 16x16 pixel blocks (SE meters a mip) and adapts in a 16-byte state buffer; color_correction.wesl multiplies the HDR frame by 2^-comp before the tone shader. Only dispatched in Auto. Env `SE_PHOTOMODE` for tests.
- Checked: unit tests of the modes and the adaptation model; Sun from 3 Gm in Auto (auto_sun.png) shows sunspots and a dim corona against HDR (hdr_sun.png); Earth view stays as HDR (median under 0.35 -> comp 0). 8 still Auto frames identical apart from the pre-existing overlay/star changes. Frame time Auto 7.9 ms vs HDR 7.4 / main 8.1.
