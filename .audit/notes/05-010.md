# 05-010 ring shadow on planet, sky (done) and clouds / rings winter (not ported: other owners' files)
- SE: rings_common.glh RingsShadow: texU from the point projected along the light onto the ring plane (`p - L min(p.y/L.y, 0)`), density `RingsParams.z x FrontMap.a`, opacity `1 - (1 - density)^(1/max(cosPhi, 0.333))`, x `clamp(min(u,1-u)/fwidth(2u))` (edge AA), x `smoothstep(0.001, 0.003, cosPhi)` (equinox). planet.glsl:1059-1073 and sky.glsl:247-253 (RINGS) call it per light. FUN_14044fe70 sets the sky's RingsParams.z = (AtmoRingsShadow fade)^2 x the ring density; the density is the ring's +0x1b58, written as `PlanetShadow` (alias `Shadow`, FUN_14046cef0; FUN_1404eb190 writes it as PlanetShadow); without one FUN_140508600 derives it as Density x (1, or 0.5 for its 20 % thin rings).
- Ported: planet.wesl `ring_shadow` is SE's RingsShadow line by line (all lights, as before). The port's ring shadow read `geom.z` of the ring material, which had become the detail-noise frequency (2000), so any ring with alpha > 0.0005 cast a 95 % black band (Jupiter's faint ring drew a black line); `RingUniform.geom.z` is now that density, `PlanetShadow` or SE's derived default (rings.rs `planet_shadow`; the thin-ring draw from the ring's seed, SE uses its loader's random stream); the detail frequency moved to `hapke2.y`. sky.wesl: RingsShadow per light at the shell point (SE's FragPos), ring texture bound to the sky material; `AtmoRingsShadow` switch (05-016).
- Checked: Saturn 2017 from the south (.audit/work/rings/c_sat_south.png: B/Cassini/A ring shadow bands on the southern hemisphere; HEAD had it all black), from the north (only the faint C ring shadow shows, sat2017.png), Jupiter (c_jup... jup_triple.png: no black ring line).
- Not ported (files of others): clouds.wesl has no ring texture binding (terrain.rs CloudMaterial would need the ring texture + `ring`/`ring_normal` like PlanetMaterial; then clouds can call the same RingsShadow). Rings winter (planet.glsl:513-533, 949-955: clouds and surface diffuse faded on the winter hemisphere by tan(sun elevation over the ring plane) x RingsWinter) needs planet.wesl outside `ring_shadow` and a terrain.rs uniform.
